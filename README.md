## Description

**Word Games** is a classic yet addictive **word search puzzle game** that challenges your vocabulary and pattern recognition skills. Drag or swipe your finger across the letter grid to connect adjacent letters and form hidden words from fun themed categories.

As you find each word, it gets highlighted with a vibrant colored line connecting the letters—providing satisfying visual feedback and making progress feel rewarding. The lines are drawn smoothly between letters (horizontally, vertically, diagonally, and even backwards), with the core complexity lying in accurately managing these connection lines for precise touch input, overlapping paths, and colorful animations without performance issues.

Enjoy beautiful scenic backgrounds, intuitive mobile-optimized controls, and hundreds of hand-crafted puzzles across diverse themes like colors, fruits, household items, and more.
